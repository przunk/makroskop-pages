01Publisher and contact

This policy describes data handling in Makroskop for Android (com.przunk.makroskop). Makroskop is developed and published by Przunk. For privacy questions, contact przunk@gmail.com.

Makroskop records meals, calculates calories and nutrients, and compares them with your goals. It requires no account and runs no server holding your diary. The app contains no advertising and does not sell your data.

02Data stored on your device

Private app storage holds products, nutritional values, barcodes, favorites and recent use. Diary records include dates, meals, product amounts, entry timestamps and saved nutritional values. The app also stores nutritional goals and their history, diary quality marks, and appearance, language and statistics preferences.

These records support your diary, calculations, statistics and preferences. Diet and goal information may reveal information about your health. Makroskop does not transmit your diary or goals to the publisher, Open Food Facts or Google ML Kit. System backups are described below.

You do not need to provide your name, email address or location to use the diary. Entering meal and goal information is voluntary; without it, the app cannot calculate your summaries. Calculations and statistics display information. Makroskop does not make automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects, or create advertising profiles.

03Open Food Facts searches

Online product search uses Open Food Facts. After a short pause in typing, the app may automatically send your search terms to that service. Barcode lookup and fetching some details of a selected result may send its barcode. The service also receives your connection's IP address and app identification, including its version.

These requests find products and retrieve their details. Avoid entering information you do not want to send to the service. Saved diary records and local products remain available offline. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy for the service's data practices.

04Photos from Open Food Facts

The app downloads packaging thumbnails and previews from Open Food Facts (OFF) for visible products from that source. Downloads default to Wi-Fi only. In settings, you can allow mobile data or turn downloads off. OFF receives the product code or image address, connection IP address and app identification. This also applies when downloading photos for products viewed from local lists or diary history. The app does not send diary records, goals, your own photos or label text with these requests.

Downloaded photos and their references are stored in the app's private cache, which has a size limit. Turning downloads off leaves previously saved photos available; a separate control clears the OFF photo cache. Android can also clear it. This cache is not part of the diary database or system backups. Downloading a photo does not change the product's saved nutritional values.

05Your own product photos

You can attach your own photo to a saved product using the system image picker. The app stores a resized, correctly oriented copy in its private files, without the photo's original metadata (EXIF). It does not upload the copy to OFF or the publisher. The gallery original stays unchanged.

You can replace or remove the copy in the product preview. Clearing the OFF photo cache does not remove your own photos. Removing a product from the list does not delete a photo linked to its earlier diary entries; you can still manage it by opening such an entry. The copy stays in app data until you remove it separately or clear app data. Your own photos may be included in the system backup described below.

06Camera and label recognition

Camera access is optional and used for scanning barcodes or taking label photos. You can refuse or revoke access in Android settings and enter data manually. The system photo picker grants access to your selected image without general access to your gallery.

The Google ML Kit library recognizes barcodes and label text on your device. Images and recognized text are not sent to Google for recognition. The app creates a bounded image copy without metadata for recognition and keeps it in private cache. A photo used only for label recognition is not attached to the saved product. The gallery original stays unchanged.

The working copy is deleted when you close recognition, apply the result or replace the image. When you next open label recognition, the app also removes unused copies older than 24 hours, keeping the file needed to resume a previous recognition session. Cleanup does not happen exactly after 24 hours. Android may clear the cache earlier; system backup excludes it. Recognized text and selected values may temporarily remain in data used to resume an open form.

07Google ML Kit technical data

Google ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage data: device and app information, installation identifiers, performance, recognition configuration, events and errors. This excludes image content and recognized text. Google uses these metrics to maintain and improve the library and prevent abuse; they are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. The app has no separate switch to disable this transmission. See the ML Kit terms, description of collected data and Google Privacy Policy.

Under its terms, ML Kit may also periodically contact Google servers for fixes, models or hardware compatibility information. The barcode and text recognition models used by Makroskop are bundled with the app.

Google states that it may process data on servers outside your country of residence. This may include processing outside the European Economic Area. Google's international data transfer information describes its transfer safeguards.

08Backups and label recognition reports

Makroskop allows Android system backup and device transfer. Depending on your phone settings, the database, preferences and your own product photos may be sent to a backup provider, such as Google, or another device. The publisher cannot access these backups. Manage backups and their deletion in your system or backup service settings.

You can choose to save a label text recognition (OCR) report as a JSON file. It contains recognized text and positions, interpretation results, image and recognition parameters, and the app version. It contains no photo or EXIF metadata. The app does not automatically send the report to the publisher. Your chosen destination may be a cloud service; review the file before sharing it. Delete an exported report separately from its chosen storage location.

09Retention and deletion

Saved local records remain until you remove them. You can delete entries and products in the app. Deleting a product does not delete its historical diary entries. Clearing app data in Android settings removes the local database, preferences and your own product photos. Uninstalling also removes local data unless the system offers and you choose to keep it. Local deletion does not remove exported reports or system backups; restoring a backup may restore data.

Android's private app storage and device protections protect local records. App connections to Open Food Facts use HTTPS. Makroskop adds no separate database password or encryption. External services apply their own retention and deletion rules to data they receive; the publisher cannot delete their copies through the app.

10Privacy correspondence

If you write to przunk@gmail.com, you provide your email address, message and any attachments you choose. This is separate correspondence with the publisher; the app does not automatically attach your diary, photos or reports. You do not need to send your diary to ask a question.

Przunk is the controller of personal data received in this correspondence. We use it to reply and handle your request. Ordinary inquiries are handled on the basis of a legitimate interest in correspondence and support (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); handling GDPR rights requests relies on a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). Messages are retained for as long as needed to resolve and document the matter; longer retention applies only where necessary for a legal obligation or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. We use Google's Gmail to operate the mailbox, under the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

11Your rights

Contact przunk@gmail.com about privacy or exercising rights available under applicable law. We cannot remotely access your diary; you manage its local contents on your device. This lack of remote access does not limit your rights against entities processing your personal data.

Where the GDPR applies to a processing activity, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing, subject to the conditions in that law. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You may also complain to the relevant supervisory authority; in Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office.

12Policy changes

We will reflect changes to data handling in this policy and update the date at its beginning. Publication of this policy or use of the app does not replace consent where consent is required for a particular processing activity.
